Cookie Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

In short

This site sets no cookies. There is no analytics, advertising, social media or tracking code on it.

It keeps a few items in your browser’s local storage, only for features you choose to use. They never leave your device, because this is a demonstration site that sends nothing.

What’s kept in local storage

Each item is essential to the feature it serves: without it, that feature can’t remember anything between pages. That is why the site has no cookie banner. There is nothing optional to agree to.

bayline.session
Keeps you signed in: your first name, your email address or mobile number, and the date you signed in. Removed when you sign out.
bayline.basket
The services and vehicle size for a wash, and the wash pack and number of packs, so the basket is the same on every page. Changes as you add or remove things.
bayline.plan
The wash pack you started: the pack, vehicle size, plate numbers, payment method (GCash, Maya or card) and start date, for your account page. Removed when you cancel the pack.
bayline.bookings
The times you booked: day, time, plate number, the services and payment method, for your account page and the washes left on a pack. Each is removed when you cancel it.
bayline.join
In this tab’s session storage, not local storage: a plate number typed on the home page, handed to the line page. It is removed the moment the line page reads it, and gone when the tab closes.

Clearing it

Sign out, empty your basket, cancel your wash pack and your bookings, or remove everything at once by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings. In most browsers it’s under privacy or site settings.

If your browser blocks site storage, the site still shows prices, wash packs and the line, but it can’t keep you signed in or remember a basket.
