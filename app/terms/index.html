This is a demonstration site

Bayline is a concept brand. This is a demonstration site: nothing you enter here is sent, and no payments are taken.

These are the terms Bayline would publish for its own site. Where a term depends on a real shop, real staff or a real payment, it also says what this demonstration site does instead.

Who we are

Bayline is a car wash and detailing brand for Subangdaku, Mandaue City, Cebu. “Bayline”, “we” and “us” mean the brand and this website; “you” means anyone using it.

No registered business stands behind this demonstration site, so it lists no business registration, tax number or office address.

Prices

Prices are in Philippine pesos and depend on the size of the vehicle: small car, medium vehicle, large vehicle or motorcycle. The price list shows which services each size can get and how long each job takes.

At the bay, the crew confirms the size before starting. If your vehicle is in a different size class from the one you chose, we tell you the new price first, and you decide whether to go ahead.

The prices on this demonstration site are sample prices for a concept brand.

The line and bookings

You can join the line for a walk-in wash by plate number, or book a start time up to seven days ahead. A booked start time is held for 15 minutes; after that, your vehicle goes into the next free bay. Ceramic coating is by appointment only and starts at 8:00 AM.

You can cancel a booking from your account at any time before it starts.

On this demonstration site, joining the line or booking a time holds no bay and sends no message. A booking is saved in your browser so your account page can show it.

Wash packs

A wash pack is a set number of washes a month: Express 4 and Express 8 hold four or eight express washes, and Care 4 holds four washes with a vacuum plus one wash with hand wax. Each pack is priced below paying for the same washes one by one, and the wash packs page shows the saving for every vehicle size.

  • Each pack covers one registered plate. For more cars, add a pack for each plate.
  • A pack covers at most one wash a day.
  • Unused washes don’t carry over to the next month.
  • Packs renew each month. Cancel from your account any time before the next month starts; the pack runs to the end of the month you paid for.

On this demonstration site, starting a pack charges nothing and nothing renews. The pack is saved in your browser so your account page can show it and count the washes left, and cancelling it there removes it.

Payment

Bayline takes GCash, Maya and cards, online for bookings and wash packs and at the bay for walk-ins, where cash is also fine.

This site never asks for a card number, a security code, a bank or wallet account number, or a PIN. GCash and Maya payments are approved in their own app, and card payments on your bank’s secure page.

On this demonstration site, no payment page opens and nothing is charged.

Your account

You sign in with your first name and an email address or mobile number, then a one-time code. There is no password. Keep your phone and email to yourself, since anyone with the code can sign in as you.

On this demonstration site, the code is shown on screen and nothing is texted or emailed. Your details stay in your browser until you sign out. The Privacy Policy lists what is kept.

Your vehicle

Please take valuables out of the vehicle before the wash, and tell the crew about anything loose, damaged or added after purchase, such as trim, wipers, antennas, roof racks or wraps.

If you see damage you think the wash caused, tell the crew before you drive off, so we can look at it with you. We aren’t responsible for items left in the vehicle or for damage that was there before the wash.

Using the site

Use the site for what it’s for: checking prices and the wait, joining the line, booking, managing a wash pack and contacting us. Don’t try to break it, overload it or reach anyone else’s details.

This demonstration site is provided as it is. Nothing on it is an offer you can accept, and using it makes no contract between you and anyone.
