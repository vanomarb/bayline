A car wash you don’t queue at

Bayline is a car wash and detailing brand for Subangdaku, Mandaue City: three bays for cars and motorcycles, open every day from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

Bayline is a concept brand, and this is a demonstration site. There’s no shop to drive to, and nothing you enter here is sent.

A line you join from anywhere

Most car washes queue at the gate, with engines idling and the road backing up. At Bayline you give your plate number on the line page, see how long the wait is, and drive over when your bay is close.

Booked times work the same way, up to seven days ahead, so a ceramic coating or an interior clean can be planned around your week.

One price list, four sizes

Small cars, medium vehicles, large vehicles and motorcycles each have their own price for every job, so you know the total before you leave home. The crew checks the size at the bay and tells you before anything changes.

Finished by hand

Every job starts with a foam wash and a rinse and ends with a hand dry. Wax is buffed by hand, seats are shampooed and drawn dry, and a ceramic coating gets the whole day.
