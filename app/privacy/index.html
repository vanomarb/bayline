In short

This is a demonstration site, so nothing you enter is sent to Bayline or to anyone else.

What you type for signing in, your basket, a wash pack or a booking stays in your own browser, and you can delete it at any time. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking.

This policy

This policy explains how Bayline’s website handles personal information. It follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission.

Bayline is a concept brand, and this is a demonstration site: no Bayline server receives anything you enter here.

What is kept, and where

Only what you enter to use a feature, and only in your browser’s local storage on this device, under these four keys:

bayline.session
Signing in: your first name, your email address or mobile number, and the date you signed in.
bayline.basket
Your basket: the services and vehicle size for a wash, and the wash pack and number of packs.
bayline.plan
Your wash pack: the pack, vehicle size, plate numbers, the payment method you picked (GCash, Maya or card) and the start date.
bayline.bookings
Your bookings: the day and time, plate number, the services, and the payment method you picked.
bayline.join
This tab’s session storage: a plate number typed on the home page, handed to the line page and removed as soon as it reads it.

The Cookie Policy lists the same keys with when each is removed. The site never asks for card numbers, security codes, bank or wallet account numbers, PINs or passwords.

What isn’t kept

The contact form checks what you type, shows a confirmation and then forgets it. Joining the walk-in line keeps your plate number only while the page is open. A plate typed on the home page is handed to the line page through this tab’s session storage, never the address, so it isn’t in the address bar or your history, and it is removed the moment the line page reads it.

Why it is kept

To make the features you use work: to keep you signed in, remember your basket between pages, and show your wash pack, the washes left on it and your bookings on your account page. Nothing is used for marketing, profiling or automated decisions.

When Bayline’s own site handles these details, it does so to give you the service you asked for, such as a booking or a wash pack.

How long it is kept

Until you remove it: signing out removes your sign-in details, removing items or booking clears the basket, cancelling a pack removes it, and cancelling a booking removes it. Clearing this site’s data in your browser settings removes all of it at once.

Sharing

Nothing is shared, because nothing leaves your browser. No analytics, advertising, social media or tracking services run on this site, and its fonts and photos are served from the site itself.

Keeping it safe

Since the details stay on your device, anyone who uses the same browser profile can open your Bayline account page. On a shared phone or computer, sign out when you finish.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to be informed, to access your personal data, to object, to have it corrected, erased or blocked, to data portability, to be paid damages, and to file a complaint.

On this site you can use most of these yourself: your account page shows everything kept about you, and signing out, cancelling your wash pack or bookings or clearing site data erases it. To complain, contact the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.

Data protection officer

Bayline’s own site would name its data protection officer here, with a way to reach them, and register with the National Privacy Commission where the law requires it. This demonstration site holds no personal data anywhere but your browser, so there is nothing for anyone to look up, correct or hand over.

Other questions about this policy can go through the contact form.
