How packs work

  • Each pack covers one registered plate. For two cars, add a pack for each plate.
  • At most one wash a day on a pack.
  • Unused washes don’t carry over to the next month.
  • Cancel from your account any time before the next month starts. The pack runs to the end of the month you paid for.

Start a pack from your basket: add the plate number it covers and choose GCash, Maya or card.
