FAQ

Do you wash motorcycles?

Yes. Express wash is ₱100, wash and hand wax ₱220, engine wash ₱150 and ceramic coating ₱4,500. Wash and vacuum, glass and interior work are for cars only.

How is the wait worked out?

It’s the time until a bay frees up for someone who joins the line now. It counts the minutes left on the cars in the three bays and the cars already waiting.

Can I leave and come back?

Yes. Join the line with your plate number, then wait wherever you like. You get a text when your bay is ready.

Do I need to book ceramic coating?

Yes. Ceramic coating takes a full day, so it’s by appointment. Drop the car off at 8:00 AM and it’s ready by 4:00 PM.

How do the wash packs work?

A pack is a set number of washes a month for one registered plate, priced below paying per wash. Express 4 is ₱360 to ₱940 a month depending on the vehicle, Express 8 is ₱680 to ₱1,770, and Care 4 (four washes with a vacuum and one with hand wax) is from ₱1,410. A pack covers one wash a day at most, unused washes don’t carry over, and you can cancel before the next month starts.

How can I pay?

Walk-ins pay at the bay once the work is done, by GCash, Maya, card or cash. Booked times and wash packs are paid when you make them, by GCash, Maya or card.
