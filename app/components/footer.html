About this site

Bayline is a concept brand, and this is a demonstration site. There is no Bayline car wash to drive to.

Everything on it works the way the shop’s own site would: the line, bookings, wash packs, the basket and signing in. All of it stays in this browser. Nothing you enter is sent, no codes are texted or emailed, and no payments are taken.

Your sign-in, basket, wash pack and bookings are kept in this browser’s local storage until you sign out, remove them or clear the site’s data.
